- Add by RSS keeps one episode across your devices. Web and the phone give those feeds their own
  pages, including music, livestreams, chapters, transcripts, downloads, and offline playback.
  Adding
  an artist also parses its albums. Followed feeds show their names
- Subscribed and Browse use chips, including livestreams. A livestream plays in the browser,
  including HLS where the browser can play it. The player names the artist with the album, a time in
  the notes starts playback there, and a sleep timer can stop it. When something cannot play, it
  stays in the player and the button explains why
- Notifications update while you are using the site or the app, and opening a push marks that item
  read. Artist pages list verified albums. Clips and playlist rows of a removed episode stay as
  Content Removed
- Taste Profile is the listen opt-in. It builds Recommendations and, within the agreement's limits,
  public popularity. Settings lists the profile and Hidden From Taste Profile. Trial listens stay in
  the profile and out of the public lists. Sign-in is a server session you can review and end,
  including after a password change. A personal API key can use playlists, the queue, clips,
  subscriptions, Add by RSS, settings, notifications, and history, and it is shown once
- Operators: drop and recreate the database. Bootstrap then applies `0001_app_schema.sql` and
  `0001_management_schema.sql`. Set `API_EXPECTED_MIGRATION_FILENAME` and
  `MANAGEMENT_API_EXPECTED_MIGRATION_FILENAME` to those names. Rename
  `POPULARITY_TRACKING_AGREEMENT_VERSION` to `TASTE_PROFILE_AGREEMENT_VERSION` and
  `NEXT_PUBLIC_POPULARITY_TRACKING_AGREEMENT_VERSION` to
  `NEXT_PUBLIC_TASTE_PROFILE_AGREEMENT_VERSION` (default `2026-10-21`). Remove
  `AUTH_JWT_EXPIRATION`,
  `AUTH_MOBILE_ACCESS_TOKEN_EXPIRATION`, `AUTH_MOBILE_REFRESH_TOKEN_EXPIRATION`, and
  `NEXT_PUBLIC_SIDEBAR_GROUP_ORDER`. Session length is `AUTH_SESSION_IDLE_EXPIRATION` (31536000).
  Access tokens are `AUTH_ACCESS_TOKEN_EXPIRATION` (900). Listen records are kept
  `STATS_TRACK_EVENT_RETENTION_DAYS` (90). After the wipe, recreate the management superuser and
  billing rows. `BILLING_SANDBOX_GRANT_ALL=true` grants Premium for every sandbox purchase on a test
  deployment only
