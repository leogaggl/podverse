- While you are signed in on more than one device, the player shows what is playing there. You can
  pause, skip, seek, and move playback between them
- A media chip selects as soon as you click it, and the list shows a spinner until the new page
  arrives. Livestreams list the newest live item first, then the soonest scheduled, and hide
  scheduled items more than a day past
- Search and filters clear with an X in the field. Player settings are a More menu. On a narrow
  window, playback progress is a line along the top of the bar
- Notifications are one line with artwork and a time. Episode, track, clip, and livestream dates
  include the local time. Transcripts highlight the current line and can follow playback
- Recommendations uses the same list and grid as Subscriptions and Browse. Playlists can show All,
  Podcasts, or Music. The footer links to a Developer page
- Add by RSS stays in the library. A missing show, episode, or clip shows Not Found. Removed
  episodes stay in clips and playlists as Content Removed
