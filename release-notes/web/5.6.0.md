- Livestreams have their own chip and play in the browser. A time in the notes starts there. If
  playback fails, it stays in the player and the button tells you why
- The player names the track with its artist and album, and a sleep timer can stop it
- Taste Profile records what you listen to, powers Recommendations, and lets you hide a show
- The notification bell and inbox update while you are here
- Signed-In Devices lists this account's sessions. Removed episodes stay in your clips and playlists
  as Content Removed
