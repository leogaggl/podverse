- While you are signed in on more than one device, the player shows what is playing there. You can
  pause, skip, seek, and move playback between them
- Subscribed and Browse are chip lists, and Add by RSS lives in one library with them. The sidebar
  marks the page you are on
- Livestreams play in the browser. The player names the artist and album, a time in the notes
  starts there, and a sleep timer can stop it. If playback fails, it stays in the player and the
  button tells you why
- Taste Profile records what you listen to, powers Recommendations, and lets you hide a show
- The notification bell updates while you are here. Signed-In Devices lists your sessions, and
  ending one elsewhere signs this page out right away
- A public playlist opens even when you are signed in as someone else. A missing show, episode, or
  clip shows Not Found. Removed episodes stay in your clips and playlists as Content Removed
