- Pressing Play starts the episode. When something cannot play, it stays in the player and the play button explains why, with Try Again. Episodes from hosts that block website playback can play
- Press a row in the queue, or a row while editing a playlist, to play it
- Add by RSS keeps one episode in your queue on every device. Listening from your phone continues here, an episode started on one device can be switched to on the other, followed feeds show their real names, and a wrong password stays explained
- Long show notes stay formatted. A shortened Summary or About links to the full notes. Tap a time in a summary to play from there
- Your clips and playlist entries stay when an episode is removed, labeled Content Removed. Settings, Advanced lists those shows, and a screen reader reads each one once. A show that left the directory says so, and one you follow can come back under Add by RSS
- People stay their own section. The dark theme is labeled Podverse. Membership says Buy Membership for Trial and Extend for Premium. Updated Terms of Service to review and accept. Sign-up tells you when the verification email could not be sent
